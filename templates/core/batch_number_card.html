Batch Number Card

The card prints once, landscape, on a single A4 sheet, with no page header and no page footer. Fill the organization name, the owner name, the phone number, the date, and the terminal number. The first box is the last digit of the year, then the month and day. The two boxes between the X marks get the period of the year (20 for the 2020s). Print is landscape on A4.
